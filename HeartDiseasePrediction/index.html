Cardiovascular Risk Intelligence

Cardiovascular diseases are the leading cause of death globally. Early identification of risk factors through predictive modeling allows patients and clinics to take preventative measures before serious heart conditions develop.

Heart Disease

Refers to various conditions affecting heart structure and function, most commonly coronary artery disease which can block blood flow and lead to cardiac arrest.

Symptoms

Key indicators include acute chest pain (angina), shortness of breath, irregular heartbeat patterns, exhaustion, and pain or numbness in the arms or jaw.

Risk Factors

Includes non-modifiable risks like age and genetics, combined with lifestyle factors like smoking, high blood pressure, cholesterol, obesity, and diabetes.

Predictive AI

Our model evaluates multiple clinical markers simultaneously, detecting complex nonlinear correlations to accurately classify patient risk parameters.
